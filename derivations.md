# Derivations from Time + Information

Under Geometry + Nature constraints:

1. **Sequence** — Time orders events; Information records them.
2. **Memory** — Information retained across Time intervals.
3. **Rate** — Change in Information per unit Time.
4. **Irreversibility marker** — Fixed Information at a Time coordinate cannot be perfectly erased without cost.
5. **Phase reference** — Time ordering keeps the 1/3–1/3–1/3 truce synchronized across toroids.

All five remain compatible with the pinned anchor at 0 and the existing truce.
